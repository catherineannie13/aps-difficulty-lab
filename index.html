The mission

What makes a level fun for you? 🎮

Designers measure how hard a level is with APS — Attempts Per Success. Higher APS = harder level.

APS = Attempts ÷ Successes

But how hard is too hard, and how easy is boring? There's no universal answer — it's personal. So you'll figure out your own:

  • Play 10 levels (each gets harder). Collect 3 🍭 candies to clear a level.
  • After each one, rate how much fun it was ⭐.
  • Work out the APS of every level yourself.
  • Then discover your personal "fun zone" — the APS range of the levels you enjoyed most.

So… what's your fun zone? 🎯

Talk it through 💬

  1. Compare with your team: did everyone land on the same fun zone? Whose was hardest / easiest?
  2. If your teammates disagree, who is the game "right" for? How could a designer keep everyone happy at once?
  3. You played ~30 attempts. King uses data from millions of players. Why does that make their fun zone more trustworthy than yours?
  4. APS only measures how hard a level is. What else would you measure to know if it's actually fun? (time per level, do players quit, do they come back…)
